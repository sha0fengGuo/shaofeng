01 / About

Building systems that
keep looking.

I am an M.S.–Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Hefei University of Technology. I am currently advised by Prof. Jiequan Cui, and previously worked under the supervision of Prof. Richang Hong. My broader interest is multimodal intelligence that can efficiently perceive changing environments, retain relevant visual evidence, and reason reliably over long horizons.
